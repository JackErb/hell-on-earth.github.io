The Pitch

You wake up on a beach you don't remember reaching, next to a raccoon who insists he's your parole officer. Hell on Earth is a classic point-and-click adventure built in the spirit of the cartoons that used to air when everyone sane was asleep — dumb logic puzzles, dumber dialogue, and a cast of background characters who'd each deserve their own show.

No combat, no fail states, just clicking things you shouldn't and seeing what happens. Built for anyone who grew up rewatching the same six late-night reruns until they knew every line.

Status

Currently in production. First playable chapter targeted for release once the raccoon's voice lines stop making the writers laugh mid-recording.
